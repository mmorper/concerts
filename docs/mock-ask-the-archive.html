Ask the Archive — on the site

Two moves: (A) make “Ask the Archive” a first-class destination in the existing nav, and (B) give it one earned moment at the end of the scroll. Everything below fakes the real scenes so you can judge placement and voice. Glowing indigo dot = the new element.

Decisions baked in (say the word to change): label “Ask the Archive” · first-person voice · all CTAs → /ask.

B · End-of-journey moment

The invitation after the last scene

After the final Artists scene, one card on the same dark gradient. The user has just lived through forty years of shows — this is the one place a call-to-action feels earned, not sold. First-person, in the archive’s voice.

Tone is the whole game here. It’s a quiet coda to the scroll — not a popup, not a banner. It appears only when you’ve actually reached the end.
