### Draft 1 — [P0.5] Media rendition spec (PRIORITY 1, do first)

Part of #323. Depends on #338 for sizing only, not for shape.
Reason it is first: it is the contract #339/#340/#341 and every
channel adapter writes against. Without it each invents its own layout,
schema and resize.

**Base masters, derived renditions:** one master per asset, stored once,
never edited. Stills = original camera file, EXIF intact. Video = original
capture, up to 4K. Content-addressed by hash so re-ingest is a no-op.
Renditions are generated by CI and NEVER committed (pure function of
master + channel; committing = stale copy. Same treatment as public/data).

**Still renditions (channel / ratio / px):**
IG feed 4:5 1080x1350 (primary) · IG story-reel 9:16 1080x1920 ·
Bluesky 4:5 1000x1250 (<=1MB HARD LIMIT) · Mastodon 4:5 1080x1350 ·
X 4:5 1200x1500 · Threads 4:5 1080x1350 · OG 1.91:1 1200x630 (exists)

Bluesky's 1MB ceiling is the binding constraint and the reason this is a
spec not a resize call. Forces a QUALITY LADDER: re-encode at
descending quality until it fits, not one fixed setting.

Format: JPEG mozjpeg q82 universal primary; WebP where accepted;
NOT AVIF (patchy support, failure mode is a broken post). sRGB.
STRIP EXIF on output (phone GPS = unretractable privacy leak once live).
Keep copyright/credit fields only — that is what #327 needs.

**Video:** MP4 faststart (moov leading); H.264 High yuv420p ~8-10Mbps@1080p;
AAC 128k retained but muted per #341. TARGET 15-30s BASE CLIP — that is
under every channel ceiling so it publishes everywhere untrimmed
(Bluesky 60s, Reels 90s, Shorts 3min, TikTok 10min).

**Safe areas (easy to miss):** Reels/TikTok/Shorts overlay UI on roughly
BOTTOM 15% and RIGHT 12%. IG also crops feed posts to a centre square in
the profile grid. So text must sit in a safe box, not merely in frame, and
the master needs margin so a 4:5 -> 9:16 crop does not decapitate the subject.

**Therefore each asset needs focalX/focalY** — normalised focal point,
default 0.5,0.5, so crops are taken around the subject not the frame centre.
Without it crops are luck: the Nile Rodgers mock survives its scrim only
because the faces happen to sit high in frame.

### Draft 2 — [P0.6] Card colour from album art (PRIORITY 2)

Depends on #327 (whether album art may be composited at all).

Cards ground on brand purple (#4c1d95 / #14111f). Derive colour from the
post's own imagery instead.

DERIVE IT FROM ALBUM ART. Obvious source is the personal photo, but that
exists for maybe 8% of posts. ALBUM ART EXISTS FOR 100% — all 57 published
posts already carry a Cover Art Archive image WITH a `source` field:
  image: { url, source: "album", ref: "social-distortion", albumName }
Record sleeves are near-ideal input (deliberately, strongly coloured).
So colour-responsive behaviour ships at full coverage immediately, purple
becomes a genuine edge case. Personal photo takes precedence where present.

TWO GUARDS: (1) CONTRAST — extracted colour will eventually be pale
yellow that kills white text. Require >=4.5:1, fall back to purple.
(2) BRAND COHERENCE — the real risk. All-different-colours = feed becomes
a swatch book and stops reading as one publisher, which defeats the point
of a house style since recognition happens BEFORE reading. So TAKE THE HUE,
CLAMP SATURATION + LIGHTNESS TO A BRAND BAND. Cards vary in hue, share a
temperature. On photographic cards the ground is mostly scrim anyway, so
spend the derived colour on the ACCENT (rule, prompt), not the background.

sharp is already a dependency and does dominant colour.

### Draft 4 — [P3.2] Crowd-sourced imagery — FILE THEN CLOSE AS DEFERRED

File to record the reasoning; do not schedule the work.

WHY DEFERRED: the gap it targets is mostly not there. Every artist has an
image (257/257), every post has album art (57/57), 85% of venues have a
real photo (67/79). Display coverage is already ~100%. What crowd-sourcing
would add is an image OF A SPECIFIC NIGHT — exactly what it cannot provide.
Licensed photography of Irvine Meadows Aug 1987 does not exist in any
library. What exists is imagery of the ARTIST GENERALLY, which we have.

IF REOPENED: Wikimedia Commons + CC-filtered Flickr are the viable
sources; scraped IG/Twitter is not, on a public archive under a named
identity. Prefer CC0 / CC BY / public domain. AVOID CC BY-SA — share-alike
can propagate to derivative works and a card compositing a BY-SA photo may
itself become BY-SA. Every asset needs the #327 credit line, and a
different-night asset must say so on the card.

WOULD CHANGE THE ANSWER: #338 returning a much higher count of shows with
no usable personal imagery, AND a design direction that genuinely needs a
photo of the night rather than of the artist.

## #340 AMENDMENT (decided after #340 was written — NOT recorded there)

#340 (media-index.json schema + resolution ladder in image-refs.ts) must record
the source order explicitly:

  PERSONAL MEDIA IS ALWAYS PREFERRED. Where an owned photo or clip exists
  for a show, it is used. Everything below is fallback, IN ORDER:
    1. Owned media matched to that show
    2. Owned media from another show by same artist — MUST BE LABELLED
    3. Album art (current default, 100% coverage)
    4. Venue photograph
    5. Generative
    6. Brand-purple typographic card

  Rungs 2-6 are all fallbacks. Rung 2 carries the DIFFERENT-NIGHT RULE:
  a photo from another night may be used, but the post MUST SAY SO.
  Implying a photo is THE night when it is not is the fabricated-memory
  failure the voice rules exist to prevent.

  EVERY POST HAS AN IMAGE. Text-only posts are not published. Within a
  carousel, interior slides may be typographic; SLIDE 1 MAY NOT.

## DECISIONS MADE IN CONVERSATION — not written down anywhere else

1. ALBUM ART IS THE COLOUR SOURCE (see draft 2). 100% coverage, already
   has a `source` field. Better than extracting from a photo that exists ~8%.
2. ALWAYS AN IMAGE; carousel interior slides may be text-only, slide 1 may not.
3. CROWD-SOURCING IS NEAR-POINTLESS — display coverage already ~100%.
   The real work is LICENCE CLEARANCE (#327), not sourcing.
4. YOUTUBE + TIKTOK DEFERRED. Reasoning: they are the most link-hostile
   surfaces (TikTok gates bio links, strips caption links; Shorts
   descriptions collapse) and the goal is CLICKS not reach. Cadence is the
   real killer — a channel with 4 videos and 3 months silence reads as
   abandoned, worse than absent. Bluesky/Mastodon tolerate irregular; TikTok
   does not. YouTube deserves a DIFFERENT answer than TikTok: it is
   search-driven, permanent, long-tail, archival — a YouTube channel as a
   home for footage with Shorts as byproduct is coherent at low cadence.
   TikTok is the clear no. NOTE: YouTube is where Content ID actually bites
   (composition claims on live performance are routine; expect ads with
   revenue to publishers).
   LAUNCH GATE (write this down so it is not relitigated): launch a video
   channel when we can commit to >=4 posts/month for 6 months. Today that
   fails with found footage; it may pass easily with generated video.
   THEREFORE THIS IS DOWNSTREAM OF #89, not parallel to it. If generated
   video works, supply stops being the constraint and the answer flips.
5. VERB RULE WAS A GUARDRAIL, NOT A BACKFILL. All 17 uses of a watch verb
   in the 57 published notes already took a performer as object. Only
   offender was the mock hook. Rule verb-object added to voice-check.ts.
