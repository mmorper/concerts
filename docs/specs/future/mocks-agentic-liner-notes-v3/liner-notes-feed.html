38 Years of Depeche Mode

I first saw Depeche Mode at Irvine Meadows in 1985, the year they released Some Great Reward. 38 years later, I watched them fill Dodger Stadium — same band, same intensity, just 41,000 more people.

June 4th Keeps Showing Up

June 4th appears in my concert history four separate times: Howard Jones in 1985, The Smithereens in 1992, Tears for Fears in 2022, The Human League in 2026. Forty-one years, one date. I've stopped calling it a coincidence.

Pacific Amphitheatre: Five Decades

The Pacific Amphitheatre has anchored Orange County summers since before I started going to shows. Eight concerts across five decades — from Thompson Twins in 1984 to Peter Gabriel in 2023. The venue closed and reopened once in that time. I outlasted it.
