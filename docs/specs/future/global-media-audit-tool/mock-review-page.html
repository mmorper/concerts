Set your bar first

Twenty frames sampled across the whole set — best, worst, and middle. Look at them before you judge anything for real. Without this, the first show gets a different standard than the fortieth, and the resulting count means nothing.

The gates: sharp at 1080px wide · subject readable, not a silhouette · performer or venue identifiable · at least 1080×1350 after crop. When you hesitate, that's a no — a bad photo under a first-person byline is worse than no photo.
