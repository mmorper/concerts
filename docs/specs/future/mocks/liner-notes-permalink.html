38 Years of Depeche Mode

I first saw Depeche Mode at Irvine Meadows Amphitheatre in 1985, the year they released Some Great Reward. 38 years later, I watched them fill Dodger Stadium — same band, same intensity, just 41,000 more people. Around that time they had just lost Andrew Fletcher, their founding member, and the Memento Mori tour felt heavier for it. That gap between two shows contains most of my concert-going life.
